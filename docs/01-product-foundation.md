# Gastos: Product Foundation

---

## 🧭 Product Vision

> **To make tracking spending effortless and habitual for everyone.**

In a world overwhelmed by financial apps, Gastos simplifies the core act of spending awareness by letting users log expenses the way they naturally communicate—through chat, voice, or photo.

---

## 🎯 Mission Statement

> **To empower users to take control of their finances by enabling frictionless expense tracking using natural language, minimal UI, and AI-driven enhancements.**

We believe people don’t struggle with knowing they *should* track expenses—they struggle with *doing* it consistently. Gastos bridges that gap.

---

## 💡 Product Philosophy

### 1. **Frictionless Logging Is Key**

Users should be able to log an expense in under 5 seconds. Whether through typing "\$5 lunch," snapping a receipt photo, or recording a voice note, entry should feel as effortless as texting a friend.

### 2. **Start Simple, Grow Smart**

We reduce overwhelm by launching with minimal categories (or tags), simple daily breakdowns, and optional fields. Advanced features will grow only in response to clear user demand.

### 3. **Text First, Everything Else Optional**

Chat is the core interface. Everything else—buttons, modals, manual forms—comes second. This keeps the product accessible and light.

### 4. **AI as a Helper, Not a Gatekeeper**

AI helps categorize, clean inputs, and generate insights—but never blocks or overcomplicates. Low confidence? Ask for confirmation. High confidence? Auto-log.

### 5. **Progress = Retention**

Just like Duolingo shows XP and streaks, Gastos celebrates logging streaks, summaries of daily/weekly spending, and positive changes in behavior to encourage habit formation.

### 6. **Design Disappears**

The interface should feel invisible. No flashy UI. Just clarity, speed, and responsiveness.

### 7. **Respect the User's Time and Data**

* Fast loading (lazy load, image optimizations)
* Minimal clicks
* Local timezone support
* Privacy-first storage (no 3rd party ads or usage sharing)
